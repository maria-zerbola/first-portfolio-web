## The research and the reason behind the project

I developed Liberamente as my bachelor’s thesis. I followed a research methodology to
investigate the role of design in promoting health and wellbeing, focusing in particular
on Non Communicable Diseases (NCDs). I found some answers in the salutogenic and
biophilic design movements, but then decided to introduce a new term to define the
specific design field and role I envisioned.

<img src="/immagini/Liberamente_megamap.png"/>

> <h2 style="color: #ed7142;"> WELL-CARE DESIGN </h2>
> Products, services or systems designed to promote a person’s wellbeing in their everyday life which can be implemented at home and in which the person is an active and aware participant.

## The Solution
A daily practice that involves a relaxing moment
of introspection before and after sleep, guided
by a repetitive hand weaving activity that
stimulates the senses and frees the mind. The
words resulting from the reflection become part
of the structure of the fabric and follow one
another day after day like a potentially infinite
diary. The fabric created acquires a personal
value beyond the quality or usefulness which is not
given importance to.

#### WHAT I DID - strenghts

As an individual thesis, the project is a true reflection of my skills and my interests.
The topic was my own original proposal, that stemmed from a desire to explore
the contribution of design to society. This project showcases how I can follow up
on my intuition with strong analytical skills and scientific rigor.
It was quite an ambitious endeavor in which I dedicated a lot of time and energy.

#### WHAT I’VE LEARNED - progress

Firstly my main objective was to find confermations and aswers to my intuitions in
regards of the new roles design can have in addressing today’s most pressing
issues. The development of this thesis is what ultimately brought me to enroll in
my master’s. By contrast I’ve also learned the invaluable assets that teamwork
brings, that simply cannot be substituted by individual work.

#### WHAT I WOULD IMPROVE - reflections

As aforementioned I would involve a team, I would also seek experts’ opinion
rather than rely solely on literature. I did validate the design with the final users but
I would try to involve them from the beginning, specifically in framing the problem.
In hindsight I can see how I was already trying to move from a product to a service
that here I called “practice”; now that I’m familiar with service design terminology
and methods I would approach things differently.
