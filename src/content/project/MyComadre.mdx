## Scenario

By 2050, due to climate change, <strong>50% of coffee plantations will become unsuitable</strong>. Most of them belong to smallholder farmers.

## Brief

How might <strong>farmers</strong> in the tropical belt be <strong>more resilient to unpredictable conditions</strong> and continue profit from their land?
</div>

## Systemic Solution

Mycomadre decodes underground messages from non-human species, offering <strong>actionable insights</strong> to help farmers adapt their agricultural practices and <strong>foster mutualistic relationships with their crops</strong>. It supports communities through <strong>agricultural transitions</strong> during uncertain times, empowering them to make informed, collective decisions for a <strong>sustainable future.</strong>

## The Design Process

<ModalViewer />
<div class="text-columns">

### from analysis
This project starts with an<strong> analysis of Nespresso</strong> with a systemic perspective. We mapped their activities and relations using <ClickableImage text="offering maps" imageSrc="/immagini/MyComadre_Nespresso_OfferingMap.svg" />, <ClickableImage text="stakeholders map" imageSrc="\immagini\MyComadre_Nespresso_Stakeholders.svg" />  and <ClickableImage text="system map" imageSrc="/immagini/MyComadre_Nespresso_SystemMap.svg" />

Then we evaluated their sustainable impact with an <ClickableImage text="assessment tool" imageSrc="/immagini/MyComadre_Nespresso_Assessment.svg" />. We discovered <strong>issues of “fair-washing”:</strong> coffee farmers are dependent rather than empowered.

To spark the ideation phase we explored a<strong> counterfactual: “what if coffee was illegal?”.</strong> We then discovered that by 2050 many countries will in fact not be able to produce coffee, not because of regulations but due to <ClickableImage text="climate change" imageSrc="/immagini/MyComadre_climatechange.svg" />.

### to synthesis
From putting the communities of smallholder farmers at the center, with the <ClickableImage text="stakeholder map" imageSrc="/immagini/MyComadre_Stakeholders.svg" /> we shifted our focus towards a <strong>more-than-human perspective</strong>, encouraging a mutualistic relationship between the farmers and their crops.

Developing the <ClickableImage text="system map" imageSrc="/immagini/MyComadre_SystemMap.svg" /> we uncovered the opportunity of leveraging community leaders as key human touchpoint to connect sustaible efforts at an international scale with the everyday practice of the farmers.
Finally we developed our solution with the aid of <ClickableImage text="offering maps" imageSrc="/immagini/MyComadre_OfferingMaps.jpg" />, <ClickableImage text="user journey maps" imageSrc="/immagini/MyComadre_JourneyMap.svg" /> and storyboard that allowed us to gain clarity, and iterate our narrative.
</div>

#### WHAT I DID - strenghts

After the initial <strong>research</strong>, my main contributions were: guiding the <strong>ideation</strong> phase by
proposing the counterfactual and suggesting to explore mychorrizal networks as a
potential source for future technological development, inspired by solarpunk.
I led most of the tech solution and designed the <strong>form and function</strong> of the MycoSensor,
backed by research.

#### WHAT I’VE LEARNED - progress

As this was my first service project in my master journey, I’ve learned to apply the main
<strong>service design and systemic tools</strong> and maps. From my intercultural team I’ve learned to
embrace a more <strong>global perspective</strong>, and put social justice at the forefront. I’ve also had
the chance to practice <strong>knowledge sharing</strong>, a fundamental skill in teamwork that I’m
focused on developing

#### WHAT I WOULD IMPROVE - reflections

<strong>Validation is missing</strong>, even though it’s a speculative design project I would seek experts’
opinion and involve coffee farmers to understand whether this solution is aligned with
the future they desire.
There is also ample potential to <strong>develop the narrative</strong> in terms of communication. I’ve
learned this is essential and powerful when dealing with the future.
